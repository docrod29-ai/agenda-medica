# NEXUSMED ORIGINAL PRODUCT IDENTITY DIRECTIVE — MANDATORY

> Recibida del dueño el 10-ago-2026, íntegra. Extiende el Master Loop V10
> (`NEXUSMED_VISUAL_EXCELLENCE_AND_CLINICAL_INTERACTION_MASTER_LOOP_V10.md`).
> Toda corrida V10 la lee junto con la directiva V10. La especificación de
> ejecución vive en `docs/design/NEXUSMED_VISUAL_DNA.md`.

There is a critical product risk:

NexusMED currently shares too many generic visual and interaction patterns
with other AI-generated healthcare SaaS products.

This must be treated as a P0 PRODUCT IDENTITY issue.

The objective is NOT to make NexusMED prettier.
The objective is to create an ORIGINAL and immediately recognizable NexusMED
clinical product language.

A clinician should be able to see a screenshot without the logo and say:
"That is NexusMED."

## DO NOT BUILD ANOTHER GENERIC AI SAAS

Aggressively detect and eliminate:

- generic left sidebar + card dashboard composition
- grids of rounded cards
- decorative purple/blue gradients
- excessive rounded-xl / rounded-2xl containers
- generic Lucide icon circles
- excessive pills and badges
- generic AI sparkle symbols
- permanent generic "AI Copilot" sidebars
- chatbot-style interfaces for non-chat clinical workflows
- arbitrary shadows
- glassmorphism
- large empty hero sections inside the clinical product
- repetitive feature cards
- generic analytics cards
- excessive borders
- identical-looking pages
- generic component-library styling
- layouts that could belong to any SaaS product
- layouts that look automatically generated by Claude, Lovable, Bolt, v0,
  Replit or similar AI builders

Do not merely recolor these patterns. Replace them.

## CREATE A NEXUSMED VISUAL DNA

Build and document a proprietary NexusMED design language. It must include:

1. ORIGINAL APPLICATION SHELL
2. ORIGINAL PATIENT HEADER
3. ORIGINAL ENCOUNTER MODE
4. ORIGINAL CLINICAL TIMELINE
5. ORIGINAL NEXUS INTELLIGENCE INTERACTION
6. ORIGINAL RECORDING EXPERIENCE
7. ORIGINAL NOTE REVIEW EXPERIENCE
8. ORIGINAL RESULT-CLOSURE EXPERIENCE
9. ORIGINAL PATIENT COMPANION EXPERIENCE
10. ORIGINAL NAVIGATION AND TRANSITION LANGUAGE

These must feel related to one another.
Do not simply rename generic components "Nexus".

## CORE EXPERIENCE CONCEPT

ONE PATIENT → ONE CLINICAL SPACE → ONE CURRENT MOMENT → ONE NEXT SAFE ACTION

The application should organize around:
PATIENT → ENCOUNTER → CLINICAL STATE → ACTION → FOLLOW-UP
not around software modules.

## QUIET CLINICAL INTELLIGENCE

AI should feel present because the product understands context, not because
an AI panel is always visible. AI appears when useful:

- abnormal trend appears next to the relevant result
- medication discrepancy appears next to the medication
- evidence appears next to the clinical question
- deterministic score appears when its required data becomes available
- follow-up issue appears in the longitudinal timeline
- missing dose unit appears beside the medication field
- source provenance appears beside the generated claim

Do NOT move every insight into a generic chatbot.

## CREATE SIGNATURE NEXUSMED INTERACTIONS

At least five interactions must become recognizable NexusMED signatures:

- NEXUS CLINICAL TIMELINE — a visually distinctive longitudinal
  representation of care.
- NEXUS ENCOUNTER MODE — a focused clinical conversation environment that
  radically reduces UI while the physician is with the patient.
- NEXUS INSIGHT — a compact contextual intelligence primitive showing:
  WHAT · WHY · SOURCE · LIMITATION · ACTION
- NEXUS CONTINUITY — navigation that always restores the clinician to the
  exact previous clinical context.
- NEXUS COMMAND — a universal Cmd/Ctrl+K clinical navigation/action system.
- NEXUS VISIT PACKAGE — the physician-approved bridge from encounter to
  patient continuation.

Do not implement all five superficially. Make them genuinely excellent.

## SYMBOL AND ICON IDENTITY

Do not use: generic sparkle as the main AI symbol · generic medical cross as
the entire brand identity · generic brain icon · generic robot icon · generic
chat bubble · generic heartbeat line everywhere.

Design a restrained symbolic language representing:
CONNECTION · CLINICAL CONTINUITY · INTELLIGENCE · PROVENANCE · ACTION

It must work at 16px, 24px, app icon, favicon, mobile home screen, desktop
navigation, loading state, AI intelligence state.

Do not redesign the legal/company brand name automatically. Create proposals
and document them separately if brand work is required.

## LAYOUT IDENTITY

Stop treating every screen as a dashboard. The data structure determines the
visual structure:

schedule → temporal canvas/list · patient → longitudinal clinical space ·
encounter → focused workspace · results → trend/action workspace ·
medications → structured clinical list · timeline → chronological narrative ·
documents → document workspace · tasks → closure queue ·
patient companion → mobile care journey

## VISUAL HIERARCHY

90% of the experience should be visually calm. Clinical hierarchy:

1. patient/current context
2. clinically important information
3. next action
4. secondary information
5. administrative controls

Administrative UI must never visually overpower clinical information.

## COLOR

Original NexusMED semantic palette. Majority neutral. Color exists for:
brand identity · selection · status · clinical risk · meaningful intelligence.
Never use different colors merely to make cards interesting.
Never represent risk only by color.

## TYPOGRAPHY

Consistent roles for: patient identity · clinical title · section · clinical
body · metadata · numeric data · critical value · AI provenance.
Avoid arbitrary bold text and arbitrary sizes.
Use typography + spacing before adding containers.

## MOTION

Restrained. Motion communicates: continuity · state change · causality ·
completion · context. No decorative looping animations.

## COMPETITIVE DESIGN REVIEW

Study Abridge, Nabla, Suki, Microsoft Dragon Copilot (public behavior).
DO NOT COPY THE SCREEN. Document:
USER PROBLEM → THEIR PRINCIPLE → THEIR LIMITATION → NEXUSMED ORIGINAL SOLUTION.
NexusMED must develop its own product grammar rather than imitate their
surface appearance.

## SCREENSHOT IDENTITY TEST — THE LOGO-OFF TEST

For every critical screen: temporarily hide the NexusMED name, logo and
obvious branding text. Ask: could this screenshot plausibly be from any
generic healthcare SaaS? If YES: the screen fails V10. Redesign until a
distinctive interaction/layout language remains visible without the logo.

Record per screen: IDENTITY_SCORE 0–10 · GENERIC_AI_SCORE 0–10.
Targets: IDENTITY_SCORE >= 9 · GENERIC_AI_SCORE <= 1.
Do not inflate scores. Use screenshots as evidence.

## THREE-SECOND TEST

Within ~3 seconds a clinician should understand: WHERE AM I? WHICH PATIENT?
WHAT IS HAPPENING? WHAT NEEDS MY ATTENTION? WHAT CAN I DO NEXT?
If not: redesign hierarchy.

## CLINICIAN ATTENTION TEST

For every element: does this deserve physician attention now?
If NO: remove, defer, collapse or move it.

## ORIGINALITY RED TEAM

An independent design reviewer specifically hunts: Claude-generated aesthetic
patterns · SaaS template patterns · similarity to competitors · unnecessary
cards · weak hierarchy · inconsistent spacing · generic iconography ·
nonclinical decorative elements · interfaces that depend on branding to look
distinct. The implementing designer cannot approve its own originality.
Every failed originality review becomes a tracked V10 defect.

## DO NOT SACRIFICE FUNCTION

Aesthetic redesign must never: remove required clinical data · hide patient
identity · hide provenance · remove safety warnings · degrade accessibility ·
increase medication error risk · lose draft data · add unnecessary clicks ·
worsen latency · break mobile · break V7/V9 workflows.

Beauty is a constraint on top of clinical excellence, not a replacement.

## FINAL PRODUCT STANDARD

The intended reaction is not "this looks like Abridge/Nabla/Suki/an
AI-generated medical SaaS". The intended reaction is:

"I have never used a medical application organized like this."

and after repeated exposure:

"That is clearly NexusMED."
